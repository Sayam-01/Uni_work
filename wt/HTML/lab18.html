Popular Places in Nepal

Pokhara is a beautiful lakeside city known for its stunning views of the Annapurna range, Phewa Lake, and adventure activities like paragliding.


Lumbini is the birthplace of Lord Buddha and a UNESCO World Heritage Site, attracting pilgrims and tourists from around the world.


Chitwan National Park is famous for wildlife safaris, offering the chance to see rhinos, tigers, and various species of birds.
